Flavor Isle, in plain facts

Hours, address, prices and answers — current as of November 9, 2026

Flavor Isle is a family-owned burger and shake diner at 103 N Main St in Smiths Grove, Kentucky, about 0.7 miles off I-65 Exit 38. It has been serving the same corner since 1964: hand-patted burgers, fresh-cut fries, and thick shakes, eaten at the counter, in the dining room, or from the walk-up window.

What we serve

  • Burgers — single, double, bacon, mushroom Swiss, and minis, hand-patted from fresh, never-frozen beef.
  • Chicken and handhelds — tenders, sandwiches and the rest of the handheld lineup.
  • Sides — french fries, curly fries, tots, onion rings and the usual diner sides.
  • Shakes — thick shakes and soft serve, spun to order. Soft serve only, no scooped ice cream.
  • Combos and drinks — combo meals, fountain drinks, and half-price drinks during happy hour.

The live menu, with today's prices, is always at flavor-isle.com/menu.

Getting here

Take I-65 to Exit 38, turn toward Smiths Grove, and the diner is 0.7 miles up on North Main Street. Flavor Isle is about 15 minutes north of Bowling Green, which makes it a natural stop for travelers, Mammoth Cave visitors and Corvette Museum traffic. There is easy parking near the diner, including room for trucks and trailers.
